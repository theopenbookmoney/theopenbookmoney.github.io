Meet the Family

Stated plainly: The people below are inspired by real people and real money problems — the author of this site lives inside this family, and we don't say which one. Names, places, and details have been changed to protect them. The situations are real life; the numbers are always run honestly.

Money is easier to understand through people than through lectures. So instead of writing at you, we write about them — one family in Saskatoon, inspired by real people at every stage of financial life. Find the one who feels like you, and follow their stories.

Follow their money as it moves: The Family Scoreboard — what each household owns, what they owe, and how every big decision changes their net worth.
