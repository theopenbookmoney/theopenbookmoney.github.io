Meet the Family

Fiction, stated plainly: Every person below is invented. They're characters in stories we write to make money make sense — not real people, not based on real people. The numbers in their stories are real; the people are not.

Money is easier to understand through people than through lectures. So instead of writing at you, we write about them — one fictional family in Saskatoon, at every stage of financial life. Find the one who feels like you, and follow their stories.

Starting out

Jasmine, 24 — The Beginner

Rob's younger sister. Barista by day, delivery driver on weekends, renting with roommates. Smart, funny, allergic to lectures — she learns by doing things wrong first. Her question: "Where do I even start when I'm broke?" Her stories: Investing, Without the Jargon · Your TFSA Is Not a Savings Account · Where Does Your Paycheque Actually Go? · The FHSA

Building

Rob, 38 — The Anchor

Night-shift supervisor at a distribution warehouse. Married to Priya, two kids, a mortgage, a minivan. Spent his twenties ignoring money; now he's catching up in public so you can learn alongside him. His question: "Am I behind, and what do I fix first?" His stories: Your Paycheque Has a Leak · The Emergency Fund: How Much Is Enough? · New Car vs Used Car: The $10,000 Question · Free Money for Your Kid's Education

Priya, 36 — The Planner

Rob's wife, a nurse. Came to Canada at 19 with no safety net and learned the financial system from zero by reading everything. Keeps the household spreadsheet. Zero patience for jargon. Her question: "How do we make two incomes cover everything without fighting about money?" Her stories: Free Money for Your Kid's Education · Your Paycheque Has a Leak

Rebuilding

Sam, 45 — The Restarter

Rob's coworker and friend. Divorced at 43, started over with half a retirement fund and a rented townhouse. Rebuilding with urgency and no tolerance for fluff. Proof it's not too late. His question: "I lost ten years — how do I catch up without gambling?" His stories: Net Worth: The One Number That Actually Matters · Your Credit Score, Decoded · The Capital Gains Tax Hike That Never Happened · Rent vs Buy

Almost there

Nadia, 58 — The Almost-There (new)

Frank's younger sister, Rob's aunt. A school division administrator with two grown kids and a condo nearly paid off. She did everything she was supposed to — and now, seven years from retirement, she's doing the anxious math out loud. Her question: "We did everything right — is it going to be enough?" Her stories: She's living the CPP-timing decision right now. Her first stories are on the way.

Enjoying it — wisely

Elena, 67 & Frank, 70 — The Realists

Rob and Jasmine's parents. Frank retired from the railway with a modest pension; Elena worked retail. Comfortable, not rich — and honest about what they'd do differently. Their hindsight is the most valuable thing on this site. Their question: "Did we save enough, and how do we make it last?" Their stories: How Much Do You Need to Retire? · Take CPP at 60 or Wait Until 70? · The 45-Minute Document That Protects Everything

The next generation

Maya, 8 & Liam, 5

Rob and Priya's kids. They don't lead stories yet — they're the reason for half of them. Every RESP dollar, every life-insurance premium, every will is about these two. Their story: Free Money for Your Kid's Education

New here? Start with whoever feels like you — then follow them. They age, they learn, they mess up, they recover. Just like the rest of us.
