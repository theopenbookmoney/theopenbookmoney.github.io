The Family Scoreboard

Updated October 20, 2026

Money advice is easy to nod at and forget. Watching a number move is different. So here it is in the open: what every household in the Open Book family owns, what they owe, and the one number that tells the truth — their net worth. Every time someone in the family makes a money decision in an article, this page moves.

Stated plainly: This family is inspired by real people and real money problems — the author of this site lives inside this family, and we don't say which one. Names, places, and details have been changed to protect them. The situations are real life; the numbers are always run honestly, with the same calculators you can use yourself.

The clock starts now — October 2026. No back-history, no cherry-picked starting point. This is the starting line, published before a single new decision lands.
