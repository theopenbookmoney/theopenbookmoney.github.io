How this works

Canadian mortgages compound semi-annually, not monthly. The calculator converts each annual rate to its exact monthly equivalent, then builds the month-by-month payment schedule for the term.

Both scenarios use the same balance, term, and remaining amortization — the only difference is the rate. Payments are monthly, fixed for the term.
