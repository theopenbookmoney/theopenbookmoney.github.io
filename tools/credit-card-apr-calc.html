How this works

Minimum payment is estimated as the greater of $10 or 3% of the current balance each month — the most common Canadian formula. Real cards vary; some use 2% or a flat $10.

Interest accrues monthly at APR ÷ 12 on the remaining balance. No new purchases are added — this shows the cost of the balance you already have.

Educational estimate: actual payoff depends on your card's exact minimum formula, daily compounding, and fees.
