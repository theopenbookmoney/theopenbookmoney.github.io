How this works

The 50/30/20 rule: split your monthly take-home pay into three buckets — 50% for needs, 30% for wants, 20% for savings. It is a starting point, not a law.

What counts where:

  • Needs — rent or mortgage, groceries, utilities, transport, insurance, minimum debt payments.
  • Wants — restaurants, clothes, streaming, hobbies, travel. Everything optional.
  • Savings — emergency fund, investing, extra payments against expensive debt.

When the rule does not fit — very low income, very high rent, a debt emergency — give every dollar a job yourself instead. That is zero-based budgeting, and it fits any life.
