How this works

The 25x rule: a rule of thumb from a 1994 study of US markets. It says you can take about 4% of your savings per year in retirement. Flip it around: multiply the yearly gap by 25.

your number = 25 × (yearly spending − yearly pension income)

The projection: your savings today grow at the assumed return for the years left, plus the future value of every monthly contribution.

projected = savings × (1 + rate)years + monthly × growth of annuity

Educational estimate: assumes a steady return with contributions at the end of each month, in today's dollars, before fees and taxes. Real returns vary. The 25x rule is a starting point, not a guarantee.
