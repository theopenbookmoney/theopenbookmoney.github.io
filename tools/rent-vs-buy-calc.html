Assumptions
  • Both start with the same cash: down payment + closing costs. The renter invests it on day one.
  • Every month, the renter invests the difference when owning costs more than rent — and draws down when rent costs more.
  • Property tax, maintenance and insurance rise 2% a year; rent rises at your rent-inflation rate.
  • CMHC insurance (2.8–4% by loan-to-value tier) is added to the mortgage when the down payment is under 20%.
  • Selling costs are deducted from the buyer's final equity. Principal-residence capital-gains exemption is reflected in the untaxed home equity.
  • Investment returns are pre-tax and smooth — real markets wobble. This is a comparison engine, not a prediction.

Educational tool only, not financial advice. Figures are estimates — your lender, insurer, and province will have their own numbers.
