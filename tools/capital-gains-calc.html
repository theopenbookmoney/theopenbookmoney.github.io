How this works

The 50% inclusion rate is the verified 2026 Canadian rule: half of your capital gain is added to your income. The proposed 66.67% hike was cancelled in March 2025 and never became law.

Your marginal rate is yours to set — it is your combined federal + provincial rate on your next dollar of income.
