How this works

Real Canadian inflation: uses the annual-average all-items Consumer Price Index (2002 = 100) from Statistics Canada, table 18-10-0005-01. The math is simple: converted = amount × (CPI of the "to" year ÷ CPI of the "from" year).

Pick your own rate: this is a projection, not a prediction. Nobody knows future inflation. The math: what your money buys later = amount ÷ (1 + rate)years.

Educational estimate only. Prices don't rise evenly — rent, food and energy move at different speeds, and your own experience will differ.
