How this works

The free grant: the Canada Education Savings Grant adds 20% of your yearly contribution, up to $500 a year, with a $7,200 lifetime maximum per child (Government of Canada figures, verified at writing). Contributing more than $2,500 in a year earns no extra grant that year.

The growth: each year's contribution plus its grant compounds once a year at the rate you set. Contributions are assumed at year-end.

Educational estimate: ignores the extra grant for lower-income families, grant catch-up room, taxes on withdrawal, and fees. Growth is an assumption, not a promise.
