What's included — and what isn't
  • Included: 2026 federal + provincial/territorial income tax brackets and basic personal amounts; CPP/QPP (5.95% / 6.40%) and CPP2/QPP2 (4%) to the 2026 ceilings; EI premiums (1.63%); federal tax credits for CPP/EI contributions; Quebec's 16.5% federal abatement.
  • Not included: Ontario surtax and health premium; Quebec QPIP premiums; the Canada employment amount and other credits (medical, tuition, donations…); employer deductions (pension, union dues, benefits). Real paycheques will differ slightly — usually in your favour.
  • Figures are estimates for education, not tax advice. Verify with CRA payroll tables or certified tax software before filing.
