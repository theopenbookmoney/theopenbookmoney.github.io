How this works

Each year you take out that year's spending first, then whatever is left grows by the expected rate. Your spending rises with inflation every year, so year 10's withdrawal buys the same as year 1's.

The simple version: year-one spending = savings × rate. Year-two spending = year-one × (1 + inflation). And so on.

Educational estimate: assumes a steady growth rate, withdrawals at the start of each year, shown as nominal value before fees and taxes. Real returns vary — this is a planning sketch, not a prediction.
