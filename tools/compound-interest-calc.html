How this works

The delay test: the late starter invests more every month — exactly enough that both sides put in the same total dollars. The only difference left is time.

catch-up monthly = your monthly × years ÷ (years − delay)

The core idea:

future value = principal × (1 + rate)time

The calculator also adds the future value of every monthly contribution and adjusts the effective monthly rate to match the selected compounding frequency.

Educational estimate: assumes a steady rate with contributions at the end of each month, shown as nominal value before fees, taxes and inflation. Real returns vary.
