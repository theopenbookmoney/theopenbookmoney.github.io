Why it matters

A single year of 2% inflation is background noise. A decade of it adds up fast — $100 in 2016 buys about what $128 buys today. That's why the inflation calculator uses real CPI data going back to 2000: to show what inflation actually did to money over time, not what a textbook says it might do.
