Milestones along the way

Compounding earns a return on earlier returns.

Your starting money can grow, and then its growth can also grow. Regular contributions add more money for the same process to work on. That is why time can matter as much as the rate.

This is an educational estimate. It assumes a steady rate and contributions made at the end of each month. Real returns vary, and inflation reduces future purchasing power.

The core idea future value = principal × (1 + rate)time

The calculator also adds the future value of every monthly contribution and adjusts the effective monthly rate to match the selected compounding frequency.
