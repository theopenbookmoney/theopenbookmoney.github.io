Which account wins for you?

The whole decision is one math question: your tax rate now versus your tax rate later. Plug in your numbers.

Comparison basis

“Equal after-tax cost” means the same dollars leave your pocket either way — the RRSP contribution is larger because your tax refund goes straight back into it. That's the fair, apples-to-apples comparison. “Equal contribution” puts the same headline dollars in each, which understates the RRSP.

  • Results are nominal, assume a constant annual return net of fees, end-of-year deposits, constant tax rates, full deductibility of RRSP contributions, and a single tax rate on all RRSP withdrawals.
